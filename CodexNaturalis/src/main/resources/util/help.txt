<show hand> Shows the cards in the hand.
<show objective> Shows the secret objective.
<show ground> Shows the common ground:
                    -Common Objectives
                    -Decks
                    -Ground Cards
<show board "idx"> Shows the board of the "idx"th player.
<show board> Shows your board.
<show card "boardIdx" "x" "y"> Shows the card placed in ("x","y") in the "boardIdx" board.
<show card "x" "y"> Shows the card placed in (x,y) in your board.
<show leaderboard> Shows the current leaderboard based only on players' points.
<flip card "idx"> Flip the hand's card in position "idx".
<place card> Asks for index and relative position of the card you want to place.
<draw card> Asks for the index of the item you want to draw:
                    -0: Resource Deck
                    -1: First Resource Card
                    -2: Second Resource Card
                    -3: Gold Deck
                    -4: First Gold Card
                    -5: Second Gold Card
<chat "nick"> Shows the chat between you and the "nick" player.
                    ("nick" = All -> chat broadcast)
<message "nick"> Asks for a message to be sent to "nick" player.
                    ("nick" = All -> chat broadcast)
<help> Opens this help's page.
